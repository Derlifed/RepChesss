What we collect

You can use RepChess without an account. If you create one, we store your username, a securely hashed version of your password (never the password itself) and your training progress. We do not ask for your name, email address or payment details, and we do not run analytics, advertising or tracking tools. When you log in, the site sets one strictly necessary cookie to keep you signed in; it is removed when you log out and expires after 30 days.

Accounts

Your account data is kept until you ask us to delete it. Because accounts have no email address, we cannot recover a forgotten password.

Data stored on your device

To remember your progress, the site saves your XP, streak, best drill score and which opening variations you have completed in your browser's local storage. Unless you are logged in, this data stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can delete it with the Reset progress link on the home page or by clearing site data in your browser. Clearing browser data, or switching browsers or devices, removes your progress.

Technical data and third parties

When you load the site, our hosting provider ([HOSTING PROVIDER]) may log technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request. The page also loads a chess rules library from cdnjs (operated by Cloudflare) and the Outfit typeface from Google Fonts, so those providers receive your IP address and browser details. Their handling of that data is governed by their own privacy policies.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with anyone other than the providers described above.

Your rights

If you have an account, you can ask us to access, correct or delete its data. Otherwise we do not hold personal data about you. Contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

Children

RepChess does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

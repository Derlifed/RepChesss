Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Who we are

RepChess is operated by Derlifed, an independent developer. You can contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect

You can use RepChess without an account. If you create one, we collect the email address, username and password you choose, and we save your progress (XP, streak, best drill score and which opening variations you have completed) to your account. We do not ask for your real name or payment details, and we do not run analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Data stored on your device

To remember your progress, the site saves it in your browser's local storage, whether or not you have an account. Without an account, this data stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can delete it with the Reset progress link on the home page (which also resets the copy saved to your account, if you are logged in) or by clearing site data in your browser. Clearing browser data, or switching browsers or devices, removes progress that is not saved to an account. Logging out also clears the progress held in that browser.

Accounts, cookies and passwords

When you log in, we set one cookie, rc_session, that keeps you logged in for up to 30 days. It is needed for accounts to work, scripts on the page cannot read it, and it is removed when you log out. We use no other cookies. Your password is never stored in readable form: we keep only a salted hash, so we cannot see it. We use your email address to run your account and to send you a password reset email when you ask for one. Your username identifies your account and is not shown to other users.

Technical data and third parties

We rely on these providers to run the site:

  • Vercel Inc. hosts the site and may log technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of each request.
  • Neon, Inc. hosts the database that stores accounts and saved progress.
  • Resend sends password reset emails. It receives your email address and the content of the email, and only when you ask for a reset.
  • cdnjs (operated by Cloudflare) serves the chess rules library and Google Fonts serves the Outfit typeface, so those providers receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads.

Their handling of that data is governed by their own privacy policies, and they may process it in the United States and other countries. We also keep short-lived counters, stored in hashed form, that limit repeated log in, sign up and password reset attempts.

How long we keep data

We keep your account and saved progress until you ask us to delete them. Password reset links expire after one hour. The attempt counters mentioned above are cleared out shortly after their time window ends.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with the providers described above, which handle it on our behalf, and where the law requires us to disclose it.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete the personal data we hold about you, including your account, by contacting us at [CONTACT EMAIL]. Deleting your account removes your email address, username and saved progress from our database, and copies in our providers' backups age out on their usual schedule. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

RepChess is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are for people who are at least 13 (or older, where your country sets a higher minimum age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.
